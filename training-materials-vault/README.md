## 🚀 3 Easy Ways to Upload Your Files Freely

### 1. Direct Drag & Drop into Windows File Explorer (Recommended & 100% Free)
You can directly paste or drag your PDFs and images into this folder on your computer:
- **Root Folder**: `c:\Users\Lenovo\Downloads\ai-cbse-command-center\training-materials-vault\`
- **Drop incoming new files in**: `training-materials-vault\inbox-new-files\`
- **Or organize them by subject**:
  - `training-materials-vault\maths\` (Mathematics Class 10 / RD Sharma / RS Aggarwal / NCERT Exemplar)
  - `training-materials-vault\science\` (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Tapasya booklets)
  - `training-materials-vault\social-science\` (History, Geography, Civics, Economics)
  - `training-materials-vault\topper-notes\` (Handwritten topper sheets, formula charts)

### 2. In-App Upload UI (Inside the Website)
Open the website, go to the **📂 Material Training Vault** tab:
1. Click **"Upload Book PDF / Notes"** or simply drag-and-drop your files onto the dropzone.
2. The system automatically reads the text, extracts MCQs, Case Studies, Assertion-Reason pairs, and Formulas, and adds them directly to your live practice drills.
