## 1. Concept: Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT / Thales Theorem)

### Statement
If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle intersecting the other two sides in distinct points, then the other two sides are divided in the same ratio.

### Mathematical Proof Structure (5-Mark Master Formula)
**Given:** A triangle $ABC$ in which a line parallel to side $BC$ intersects other two sides $AB$ and $AC$ at $D$ and $E$ respectively.
**To Prove:** $\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC}$
**Construction:** Join $BE$ and $CD$ and then draw $DM \perp AC$ and $EN \perp AB$.

**Proof:**
1. Area of $\Delta ADE = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times AD \times EN$.
2. Area of $\Delta BDE = \frac{1}{2} \times DB \times EN$.
3. Therefore, $\frac{\text{Area}(\Delta ADE)}{\text{Area}(\Delta BDE)} = \frac{\frac{1}{2} \times AD \times EN}{\frac{1}{2} \times DB \times EN} = \frac{AD}{DB}$.
4. Similarly, $\frac{\text{Area}(\Delta ADE)}{\text{Area}(\Delta DEC)} = \frac{AE}{EC}$.
5. Since $\Delta BDE$ and $\Delta DEC$ are on the same base $DE$ and between the same parallels $BC$ and $DE$, $\text{Area}(\Delta BDE) = \text{Area}(\Delta DEC)$.
6. Hence, $\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC}$.

**Common Board Mistake to Avoid:**
Students often forget to explicitly state the reason in step 5 (Triangles on same base between same parallels are equal in area). Examiners *will* deduct 1 mark for this omission!

## 2. Concept: Similarity Criterions

Two triangles are similar if:
1. Their corresponding angles are equal.
2. Their corresponding sides are in the same ratio.

### A. AA (Angle-Angle) Similarity Criterion
If two angles of one triangle are respectively equal to two angles of another triangle, then the two triangles are similar.
*Pro Tip from KVS:* If two angles are equal, the third angle is automatically equal (Angle Sum Property). Hence, AAA is just AA.

### B. SAS (Side-Angle-Side) Similarity Criterion
If one angle of a triangle is equal to one angle of the other triangle and the sides including these angles are proportional, then the two triangles are similar.
*Warning:* The angle MUST be the included angle between the two proportional sides.

### C. SSS (Side-Side-Side) Similarity Criterion
If in two triangles, sides of one triangle are proportional to the sides of the other triangle, then their corresponding angles are equal and hence the two triangles are similar.

## 3. Advanced Applications (Ratios of Areas)
*(Note: Often tested in HOTS / Section E Case Studies)*

**Theorem:** The ratio of the areas of two similar triangles is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides.
$$\frac{\text{Area}(\Delta ABC)}{\text{Area}(\Delta PQR)} = \left(\frac{AB}{PQ}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{BC}{QR}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{CA}{RP}\right)^2$$

**Extension:** This ratio is also equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding:
- Altitudes
- Medians
- Angle Bisectors
